The Industry Challenge
Bottlenecks in Manual Toy Attachment & Packaging Design
❌ Over-engineered Fasteners
Excessive wire ties, elastic loops, and
XPE foam inflate raw material costs &
create unnecessary packaging waste.
❌ Transit Damage & Accessory Loss
Improper attachment locations lead to
product movement, accessory
detachment, or damaged display pose.
❌ High Factory Labor Time
Complex manual tie configurations
slow down high-speed assembly line
workers, increasing cost per unit.
❌ Subjective Trial-and-Error
Packaging decisions lack historical
data analytics, resulting in lengthy
revision loops between teams.
System Features & Capabilities
Comprehensive Modules Tailored for Packaging Engineers
1. Product & Pose Analysis (YOLO)
Automated detection of doll pose complexity, joint anatomical support points,
and attached accessories.
2. Attachment Recommendation Engine
Suggests precise strap types (elastic loops, molded pulp, ties), optimal
quantities, and exact placement coordinates.
3. Risk & Drop-Test Predictor
Simulates Master Carton 10-drop and SIOC 17-drop test outcomes,
movement risk, and accessory loss risk.
4. Cost & Sustainability Optimizer
Calculates unit strap cost ($), assembly labor time estimation,
and recyclability score in real time.
